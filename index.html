Five principles

The rules behind every XQ95 product.

  • 01

    Zero unintended access pathways

    We design every system to leave no unintended way in.

  • 02

    Comprehensive host isolation

    The host stays within strict limits, so only limited data can enter network streams.

  • 03

    Zero data collection

    We collect no personal data and no telemetry.

  • 04

    Client-side data sovereignty

    Your data belongs to you and stays on your machine.

  • 05

    Local-first AI processing and zero data retention

    Built-in AI runs on your machine and keeps nothing.
