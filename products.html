Products

Software for work and for business

From a focused desktop to tools for running hundreds of machines. These products are private and built by XQ95.

Workdesk OS is in development. The other products are planned. Features listed are goals, not finished work. Every product follows our five principles.

Workdesk OS

In developmentPrivate

A desktop operating system organized around dedicated workspaces.

Each kind of work gets its own space, so switching tasks doesn't mean digging through windows.

Who it's for

People and teams who want a focused, uncluttered desktop.

Planned capabilities

  • Separate workspaces for different kinds of work
  • One consistent desktop shell across every workspace
  • Designed to stay light on memory and CPU

XQ95 Enterprise Suite

PlannedPrivate

Run XQ95 across an organization.

Central tools for IT teams to manage the people, devices, and settings in their organization.

Who it's for

IT teams and administrators.

Planned capabilities

  • Central management of users and devices
  • Policies applied consistently across machines
  • An administration console for IT teams

XQ95 Deployment System

PlannedPrivate

Install and update XQ95 on many machines from one place.

Prepare a system once, then roll it out and keep it updated across a fleet of machines.

Who it's for

Organizations setting up or maintaining many computers.

Planned capabilities

  • Prepared system images for new machines
  • Staged rollouts of updates
  • Rollback when an update causes problems

XQ95 Security Suite

PlannedPrivate

Protect XQ95 systems and the data on them.

Access control, security updates, and auditing, managed together.

Who it's for

Organizations that need to control access and prove compliance.

Planned capabilities

  • Access control and permissions
  • Security updates and alerts
  • Audit logs of important system events

XQ95 Client Solutions

PlannedPrivate

Software and support built around a client's needs.

We adapt XQ95 products to the way a client's organization already works.

Who it's for

Businesses and institutions with specific requirements.

Planned capabilities

  • Custom configurations of XQ95 products
  • Integration with existing systems
  • Ongoing support
