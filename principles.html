Principles

Our five core principles

Every XQ95 product is designed around these rules. They decide what we build, what we collect, and where your data lives.

01

Zero unintended access pathways

We design every system to leave no unintended way in.

What it means

  • We reduce the attack surface: fewer open services, ports, and permissions.
  • Every way into a system is one we chose on purpose.
  • When a bug does occur, the design limits what it can reach.

What it doesn't mean

  • It isn't a claim that our software has no bugs.
02

Comprehensive host isolation

The host stays within strict limits, so only limited data can enter network streams.

What it means

  • The host system is isolated and kept within defined limits.
  • Only a limited amount of data can enter network streams.

What it doesn't mean

  • It doesn't mean cutting features. Isolation must not reduce what the system can do.
03

Zero data collection

We collect no personal data and no telemetry.

What it means

  • No personal data and no telemetry, unless the law requires it.
  • For repair services, we collect operational data only with your consent, and never more than the consent document specifies.

What it doesn't mean

  • It doesn't mean we ignore the law. We collect what we're legally required to collect, and nothing beyond it.
04

Client-side data sovereignty

Your data belongs to you and stays on your machine.

What it means

  • You can use our products without being watched or tracked.
  • Your data doesn't leave your machine, and you keep full control of it.
  • When you permanently delete data, no copy remains with us. Any recoverable copy stays only on your own machine.
05

Local-first AI processing and zero data retention

Built-in AI runs on your machine and keeps nothing.

What it means

  • Every product with a pre-installed AI agent processes data locally.
  • No data is sent to XQ95 or anyone else.
  • Online AI tools are used only if you choose them and give consent.

What it doesn't mean

  • It doesn't mean we'll never offer online AI services. Using them is always your choice.

This website follows them too

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts, and no requests to third-party servers. Our host, Cloudflare, processes the basic request data needed to deliver these pages.
